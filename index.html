Cosmickon is a two-day creative festival by EXVERSE Club, Department of Multimedia and Animation, School of CSA, REVA University.

Step into a universe where gaming, art and storytelling collide — with an esports championship, a sketching and digital art contest, and an immersive murder-mystery game.
